## [Baltimore City 311](https://311services.baltimorecity.gov/)

**Contact for:**

- Parking concerns and abandoned vehicle complaints

- Questions about public parks and lands, or to report an issue

- Bulk trash and appliance pick up

- Construction concerns and reporting 

- Potholes and sidewalk repair

- Graffiti removal

- Water outage

- Illegal dumping and alley clean up

- Housing inspections and vacant property complaints

- ...and more!
