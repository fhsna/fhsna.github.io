Welcome to Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association!

The Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association (FHSNA) serves the neighborhood bounded by Light Street, Cross Street, Covington Street, and Fort Avenue.

FHSNA isn’t just a neighborhood. We're a vibrant tapestry of diverse individuals coming together to create something special. From community events that forge friendships to initiatives that make our streets greener, cleaner, and more beautiful, we're all about building a neighborhood we're proud to call home.

But we don't do it alone. FHSNA is all about partnerships – with local businesses, schools, and city officials – because we know that together, we can achieve so much more.

So, whether you're a longtime resident or a new neighbor — come on in, get involved, and let's make magic happen.

Please join Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association on the last Tuesday of every month for our general community meeting.

Meetings are held In-Person and over Zoom in the basement of St. Ignatius Loyola Academy at the corner of Battery and Gittings Streets. Join us for social meet and greet at 6:30pm followed by our general meeting at 7:00 pm.
