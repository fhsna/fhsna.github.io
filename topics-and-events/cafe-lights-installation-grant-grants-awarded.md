## Café Lights Installation Grant Program — Grants Awarded
FHSNA is pleased to announce that both \$500 Café Lights Grants have been awarded to neighborhood blocks to help purchase and install café lighting.

FHSNA recommends local contractor Chris Broughton, who has installed café lights throughout South Baltimore neighborhoods. He can be reached through Facebook (@Chris Broughton), by phone or text at 410-241-4696, or by email at <chrissbroughton@gmail.com>. Refer to this [Installation guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V_cvjqCpB81yBUpjO6d2ZN42Bi4oieOe/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=114792931018438261634&rtpof=true&sd=true) for more information.
