**Free Dawn-to-Dusk Light Bulb Program**

The Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association (FHSNA) has been awarded a grant from the South Baltimore Gateway Partnership (SBGP) to help increase neighborhood safety through improved outdoor lighting. Through this grant, FHSNA has purchased 300 dawn-to-dusk light bulbs that will be distributed free of charge to eligible residents.

**Who qualifies for a free dawn-to-dusk light bulb?**

Any resident who lives within the FHSNA boundaries—Light Street, Cross Street, Covington Street, and Fort Avenue—is eligible to receive a free light bulb.

To verify residency, please provide one of the following:

- A valid Residential Parking Permit Area 19 parking pass

- A utility bill or other official document showing your current address

- A driver's license with your current address

**How do the light bulbs work?**

After installing the bulb in your outdoor light fixture, turn the light switch on and leave it on. The bulb contains a built-in sensor that automatically turns the light on at dusk and off at dawn.

**How many light bulbs can I receive?**

Each household is eligible to receive one (1) free dawn-to-dusk light bulb while supplies last.

**Where can I pick up my free light bulb?**

FHSNA will host two distribution events. The first distribution event will be held during a community happy hour on July 24 from 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. at the St. Ignatius Blacktop, where 150 light bulbs will be available on a first-come, first-served basis. The second distribution event is planned for the fall, with the date, time, and location to be announced.

**What if I cannot attend the distribution events?**

Additional distribution opportunities may be announced if supplies remain available. Please watch FHSNA communications for updates.
