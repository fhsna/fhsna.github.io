### Join Us for a Dusk to Dawn Happy Hour!

The Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association (FHSNA) invites neighbors to our Dusk to Dawn Happy Hour on Friday, July 24, from 5:00–8:00 p.m. at the St. Ignatius Blacktop.

Thanks to a grant from the South Baltimore Gateway Partnership, we’ll be giving away 150 free dusk-to-dawn light bulbs to eligible residents on a first-come, first-served basis. These bulbs automatically turn on at night and off during the day, helping make our neighborhood brighter and safer.

FHSNA residents within the boundaries of Light Street, Cross Street, Covington Street, and Fort Avenue are eligible to receive one free bulb while supplies last. Please bring proof of residency (such as an Area 19 parking permit, driver’s license, or utility bill).

Stop by for a snack, a drink, and a chance to meet and catch up with your neighbors!
