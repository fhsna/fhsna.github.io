### Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association (FHSNA)
**Meeting Notes  
Date:** October 28, 2025  
**Time:** 7:00 PM

**Meeting Start:**
President **Nadine Bowen** kicked things off at **7:00 PM**.

### Neighborhood Updates (Nadine Bowen)
- **Dumpster Day** is coming up on **November 15** at *Digital Harbor High School*. Check the FHSNA website for the list of items that *can’t* be tossed.  

- **Monthly Happy Hour** – **November 19** at *Octobar*, starting at **5:30 PM**. Great chance to meet neighbors and support a local spot.  

- **Holiday Wreath Sale** – Wreaths are available to order through the website. **Pick-up** is on **December 6** from **10 AM – 1 PM**.  

- **Frosty Fest** – *Federal Hill Main Street* will host this event on **December 6**, from **10 AM – 2 PM**.  

### Treasurer’s Report (Kyle Robinson)
Kyle shared the **annual report and budget**. Copies are available at the front desk or by emailing him directly if you’d like one.

### Baltimore Police Update (Sgt. Raul Rivera)
Sgt. Rivera talked about recent crime in the neighborhood and plans to keep things safe over Halloween weekend.  
Residents are encouraged to report any concerns.  
📧 **raul.rivera@baltimore.police.org**

### Residential Permit Parking (Shamir Cole-Butler)
Shamir answered questions about **virtual parking permits**.

- New patrol car equipment is being tested and should be online soon — this should help fix ticketing issues.  

- She also reminded everyone about **visitor permits which can only be used for 3 days max in a row** and how to reach her with questions.  
  📧 **shamir.cole-butler@bcparking.com**
