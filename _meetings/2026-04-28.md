- 2 attempted Hyundai auto thefts (ignitions damaged, cars not stolen)

- 4 larcenies (1 suspect caught and cited) 

- 19 package thefts (only 2 of 12 contacted residents provided footage)

- **Police the following actions to deter crimes**

- Get free steering wheel locks at police district

- Install lockboxes for package deliveries

- Install Ring cameras (front and back recommended)

- Report all package thefts and submit surveillance footage via police link

- Post footage to community social media for suspect identification

- Questions or concerns can be directed to: raul.rivera@baltimore.police.org

**BGE Infrastructure Project Update:** Paused indefinitely due to community sentiment, political pushback, and developer changes at Port Covington. Original plans no longer align with current development needs

- **Project Details:** *Unde*rground transmission line from Green Street substation to new Port Covington substation (near Food Street, through Key Highway and Bridgely Delights). Replacing transmission line reaching end of useful life (replacement still required eventually). Originally sized for additional power capacity for harbor and port development. Existing infrastructure replacement paid by customer base; new capacity costs require further clarification from BGE project team.

- **Next Steps:** BGE creating website with project information and community survey. Return visit planned for fall to share developer feedback and community input. Opportunity to reset and re-engage community in planning process
