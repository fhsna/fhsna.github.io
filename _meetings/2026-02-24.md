**FHSNA Monthly Meeting Notes**

Date: February 24, 2026  
Time: 7:00–8:00 PM

**Neighborhood Updates – Nadine Bowen**

- Nadine announced that she will not be running for president next year. Board elections will take place in May. Anyone interested in running for a board position should contact Nadine. All open positions are listed on the association website.

- **Dumpster Day** will be held on **March 21**. Tools and trash bags will be available for neighbors to borrow as part of a neighborhood spring-cleaning effort.

- **Sail 250** events are planned for June. A representative will attend an upcoming meeting to provide additional details.

**Baltimore Police Update – Sgt. Raul Rivera**

- Sgt. Rivera of the Baltimore Police Department reviewed recent crime activity in the neighborhood.

- He reported on a recent attack involving a student from Digital Harbor High School.

- Questions or concerns for Sgt. Rivera can be directed to **raul.rivera@baltimore.police.org**.

**Chili Cook-Off Wrap-Up Report**

- Estela Martinez and Nadine Bowen reported that the event was a success, with over 250 attendees and 22 competitors. Many new cooks participated this year alongside returning competitors. Estela thanked the many volunteers and sponsors, including St. Ignatius Church and Little Havana.

- Nadine reviewed the event financials and announced the funds raised, which support association insurance, office supplies, neighborhood activities, and donations to the local schools and church.

- Next year’s Cook-Off will take place on **February 7, 2027** — mark your calendars.

**Nick Riley – Director of Operations, Liv’s Tavern**

- Nick introduced Liv's Tavern, the new restaurant now operating in the former location of Mother's Federal Hill Grille.  They are eager to support the association and be part of the community.

- Liv’s Tavern generously donated the appetizers and wine for the meeting.

**Safety Committee Update – Colin White**

- Colin shared updates on ongoing efforts to reduce theft and loitering in the neighborhood.

- He reminded residents to call 911 if they witness suspicious activity.

**City Council Update – Zac Blanchard**

- Councilman Blanchard presented his 2026 legislative priorities and distributed a flyer with details. Key initiatives include:

  - A bill to hold property owners accountable for improper trash disposal and inadequate storage receptacles

  - Updating the city code to streamline graffiti removal and protect property owners

  - Proposed reforms to the Residential Parking Permit Program

  - A zoning amendment to prevent new smoke shops from locating in residential areas

  - New tools to better fund and support Main Streets

  - Expanding opportunities for homeownership

  - Working with state partners to lighten the tax burden on homeowners

  - An electoral reform package to strengthen public campaign financing

- Zac also serves on the Charter Review Committee and encouraged residents to submit recommendations for Baltimore’s charter reform process.

- Residents are reminded that when submitting a 311 report, they should forward the confirmation code to Zac’s office so staff can track the response.

Meeting ended at 8:13pm.
