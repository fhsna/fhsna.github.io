**Baltimore Police Update (Sgt. Raul Rivera)**

- Reviewed recent crime activity in the neighborhood.

- Reported one larceny and some recent incidents involving students from Digital Harbor High School. A neighbor noted that the students involved were expelled.

- Questions for Sgt. Rivera can be directed to raul.rivera@baltimore.police.org.

**Department of Transportation Update (Qiana Gabriel)**

- Discussed the review of Riverside Avenue, including a request to add bump-outs.

- Requested feedback from neighbors regarding traffic concerns and areas needing improvement.

- Noted concerns:

  - St. Ignatius School is not currently designated as a school zone.

  - Ongoing issue of cars driving the wrong way on Riverside Avenue from Fort Avenue.

- Neighbors are encouraged to continue sharing traffic-related concerns with Monaye Easley-Smalls at monaye.easley-smalls@baltimorecity.gov and to include the relevant SR number for investigation.
