**Federal Hill South Neighborhood Association (FHSNA) Meeting Minutes  
Date:** September 30, 2025  
**Time:** 7:00 PM  

  

**Meeting Called to Order:**
FHSNA President Nadine Bowen called the meeting to order at 7:00 PM.

**Guest Speakers & Updates:**

**1. Alex Zequeira – President, St. Ignatius Loyola Academy**
Alex Zequeira shared information about the school, its students, and how the academic program works. The schools if very proud to announce that the class of 2021 had a 100% high school graduation rate this year. He also noted that several faculty members live in the neighborhood and are active members of the community.

**2. Sgt. Rivera – Baltimore Police Department**
Sgt. Rivera and his team provided updates on the local crime report and the ongoing plans related to the entertainment district. Residents were encouraged to stay informed and report concerns.

**3. Taylor Schwartz – Executive Director, Federal Hill Main Street**
Taylor Schwartz discussed current efforts to support the small business district, with a focus on safety and filling vacant commercial spaces with local owners and businesses. She encouraged residents to shop local and support neighborhood businesses.

**4. Rebecca Mark – District 11 Liaison, Mayor’s Office of Community Affairs (MOCA)  
Rebecca can be reached at 443-615-2262 or Rebecca.Mark@baltimorecity.gov**
