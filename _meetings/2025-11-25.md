### Personal Safety Tips
- iPhone users can activate SOS by pressing both volume buttons + the on/off button.  

- Keep serial numbers of valuable items; consider engraving them.  

- Trust your instincts; avoid unsafe situations (e.g., walking alone at night, wearing headphones).  

- Report suspicious individuals immediately with detailed descriptions (e.g., person holding a package that seems not to belong to them).  

- Use secure package boxes and coordinate with neighbors to prevent holiday-season porch piracy.  

- Report juvenile misbehavior to **Principal Jackson (Digital Harbor High School)** and to the police.  

### Dirt Bikers & Porch Pirates
- A resident raised concerns about dirt bikers harassing people on Light Street.  

- Sgt. Rivera advised reporting any gas stations supplying fuel to illegal dirt bikes.  

- The **Metro Mobile Unit (MMU)** task force is actively working on the issue.
