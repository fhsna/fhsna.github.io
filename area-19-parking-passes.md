## AREA 19 PERMIT(S):

More information about Area 19 Permits can be found on the [Parking Authority's website](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/parking/on-street-parking-overview/residential-parking-permits).

**Contact Information:** If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Parking Authority at <rpp@bcparking.com> or 443-573-2800, ext 863, 845, or 851. 

AREA 19  HAS VIRTUAL PASSES

Area 19 transitioned to Virtual Pass System May 1st, 2025. 

Apps are available for Apple and Android phones   -   Search for Permitconnect Baltimore in your app store.

[This is a link to a FAQ sheet](https://pabc.myparkinginfo.com/cws/pages/help#faqs) to help with questions you may have.
